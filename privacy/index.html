Privacy

01Who

This site belongs to Pol Planas, freelance web designer in Catalonia. You can reach me at pol@polplanas.com.

02What I collect

Only what you write in the contact form: your name, your business, what you need and your email. Nothing else, and no tracking cookies.

03Why

To reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to prepare a proposal. The legal basis is your consent when you send the form.

04How long

As long as needed to handle your enquiry, and no more than twelve months unless we start a project together.

05The window

When you write or draw on the glass, the shape of your strokes (only the lines, never anything about you) is sent to Google’s handwriting and drawing recognition to read it. The voice that answers is an open model that runs inside your own browser: what you write to it never leaves your device. Its files are downloaded once from Hugging Face and kept in your browser’s cache. To answer in your language, what you write and what it answers pass through a translator (Chrome’s own when available, otherwise Google Translate).

06Who else sees it

No one. The message travels through my email provider or form service, which only processes it to deliver it to me.

07Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to pol@polplanas.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
